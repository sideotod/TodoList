# Todo voice

Todo uses concise, clear Korean that helps students understand the next action and the state of a schedule.

## Writing rules

- Use short action labels such as `저장`, `변경`, `완료`, and `닫기`.
- Keep dates, times, and time zones explicit when they affect a decision.
- Distinguish `일정` from `할 일`: an event is a calendar schedule, and a task is an action to complete.
- Say where a change was saved or synced. Explain a recovery step when something fails.
- Avoid decorative slogans, vague success messages, and blame.

## Product terms

- `일정`: timed calendar event or academic period.
- `할 일`: an action shown in the daily task list.
- `디데이 정렬`: ordering upcoming progress cards.
- `완료 일정`: schedules whose period or time has ended, or that the user marked complete.
- `색상 조합`: the active accent choice: `청록`, `앰버`, or `세이지`.
